Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how users of Family Expense Manager can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your Family Expense Manager account and associated data, please send an email to:

Email: mmascrenem@gmail.com

Please use the subject:

Family Expense Manager – Data Deletion Request

Information to Include

To help us identify your account and process your request, please send the request from the email address associated with your account, where applicable.

What Happens After Your Request

We will review your request and delete applicable personal data associated with Family Expense Manager, subject to any information that we may be required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

Google Drive Data

Family Expense Manager can provide Google Drive functionality when the user authorizes Google Sign-In and Google Drive access. Files stored in the user's Google Drive remain under the user's control. Users may manage or delete their Google Drive files directly through Google Drive.
