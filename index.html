Privacy Policy for Family Expense Manager

Last Updated: August 23, 2026

Family Expense Manager ("the App") is a family expense management application designed to help users record, organize and manage family financial transactions.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App, including the optional Google Sign-In and Google Drive functionality.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The core functionality of Family Expense Manager stores information locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Family name
  • Family member names
  • Family member information entered into the App
  • Expense and transaction records
  • Current user information used within the App
  • App preferences such as theme and text size

This information is stored locally on your device using the App's local storage functionality.

2. Google Sign-In

The App provides an optional Google Sign-In feature for users who choose to use the Google Drive storage functionality.

When you choose to sign in with Google, the App uses Google authentication services to authenticate your account.

The App may receive basic account information made available by Google authentication, such as your name, email address and profile information.

Google authentication is used to provide access to the optional Google Drive functionality.

3. Google Drive

Family Expense Manager provides optional functionality that allows users to interact with their Google Drive.

Depending on the features you use, the App may allow you to:

  • View files and folders in your Google Drive
  • Create folders
  • Upload files to Google Drive
  • View file metadata
  • Delete files or folders after user confirmation
  • View certain Google Drive storage information

Files uploaded to Google Drive are stored in the user's Google Drive account and are subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

The App does not claim ownership of files that you store in your Google Drive.

4. Types of Files That May Be Uploaded

The Google Drive functionality may allow users to upload files such as:

  • Receipts
  • Images
  • PDF files
  • CSV files
  • JSON files
  • Other files selected by the user through supported functionality

Users decide whether and what files they upload to Google Drive.

5. Firebase Authentication

The App uses Firebase Authentication to support Google Sign-In for the optional Google Drive functionality.

Firebase is not used by the core expense-management functionality as a cloud database for storing your family transaction records.

6. Financial Information

The App allows users to enter expense and transaction information. Such information is stored locally on the user's device as part of the core expense-management functionality.

If a user chooses to export, upload or otherwise store information using the optional Google Drive functionality, that information may be stored in the user's Google Drive account.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

The App does not use your family expense records for advertising purposes.

Information is handled according to the features you choose to use. Core family and transaction information is stored locally on your device. Information you choose to upload to Google Drive is handled through Google's services and stored in your Google Drive account.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services necessary to provide certain functionality, including:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Google Drive API

These services are governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device, Google account and Google Drive account.

10. Data Deletion

Core family and transaction information stored locally by the App can be removed by deleting the relevant information within the App or by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App, subject to the operating system's behavior.

Files uploaded to Google Drive remain under the user's control and can be managed or deleted through Google Drive.

11. Children's Privacy

Family Expense Manager is not specifically directed toward children. Users should not use the App to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its functionality or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.
